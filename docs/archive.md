# Retained research and historical archive

The active entry point is [tb3-medical](../README.md). Seven semantic groups own
medical ideas, experiments, findings and stories. Retained medical round documents
and freeze receipts keep their original paths and bytes; the [round register](research-rounds.md)
is historical chronology, not an instruction to execute old next actions.

The [pre-medical archive](../archive/README.md) indexes the exact nonmedical and
superseded material removed from active tracking, including the original interview
publication. Use its recovery command for original paths referred to by historical
receipts. It preserves both Git provenance and verified local copies.
