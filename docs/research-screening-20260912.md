# First screening of the 100-card bank

Completed 2026-09-12 using the [recorded sourcing and screening method](research-sourcing-methodology.md).
**2 advance to bounded reproduction, 50 are on hold, and 48 are rejected in
their current form.** All 100 received a desk decision; 29 received deeper
source inspection, including 14 with local calculations or fixture checks.
These are not 100 executed baselines or new Terra failures.

**Security tasks remain excluded.** The target remains hard but less complex,
short-horizon work with one conceptual crux, ordinary tools and ample reasoning
time. No model trials, Harbor installations or benchmark environments were
launched. The local model ledger remains six valid Terra passes, zero genuine
failures and two infrastructure-only attempts.

The dated [screening ledger](evidence/broad-bank-screening-20260912.json) owns
these decisions and their evidence depth. The original
[100-card bank](evidence/broad-task-bank.json) remains unchanged as the sourcing
snapshot. Catalog statuses and next actions now reflect this screen. The
initial twelve-item preparation queue is superseded by the decisions below.

## Reproduction and limits

The [calculation receipt](evidence/broad-bank-screening-checks.json) contains
the outputs above and explicit per-check limits. Replay with Python 3.12:

```sh
.venv/bin/python scripts/research/broad_bank_screen_checks.py --sheets-archive .cache/broad-survey/sheets-400.tar.gz
```

Without `--sheets-archive`, the script runs the self-contained arithmetic,
clock-recurrence and SQL checks only. It uses the Python standard library.
The [source manifest](evidence/broad-bank-screening-sources.json) records new
downloads, reused-file hashes and the pinned spreadsheet archive provenance.
The ignored archive/cache is not shipped in a fresh checkout; obtain the exact
recorded archive and verify its hash before replaying workbook checks. No Excel
recalculation, FreeCAD execution, Verilog simulation, LLVM/Alive2 proof or full
benchmark-harness execution is claimed.

Rejection is deliberately allowed on a clearly labeled desk assessment: for
example, PostGIS already exposes the proposed clustering operation, whereas a
measured pass was not run for that card. Such judgments must not be reported as
Terra successes. Holds specify the missing input, convention or discriminating
check; they are not 50 nearly-ready tasks. The outcome is **100 sourced leads
screened**, not the desired eventual set of 100 diverse hard experiments.
