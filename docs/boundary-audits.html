03 / What changed after this round

A reviewed miss was a starting point.

Why case 32 initially led

It used the least time, output, uncached input and estimated cost among the three Sol misses. Terra/max had also missed the identical frozen task. Case 83 likewise retained an identical-task Terra miss. The v2 changes in cases 46 and 61 prevent an equivalent cross-model comparison.

Why case 32 was ruled out

Case 32 was ruled out as too nitpicky. The later reassessment questioned whether the tiny boundary edit and its tolerance formed a fair, meaningful benchmark task. It also found substantial geometric and relational analysis already present in the traces. Neither a raw zero nor elaborate tool use resolves task validity.

Keep two judgments separate: the agent missed a retained controlled alteration, and the task is suitable for benchmark promotion. The first can remain true while the second is unresolved.
