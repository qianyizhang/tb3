The expanded task: recover a material heart, then validate the mechanics

1 · A coherent myocardial body

Use calibrated videos and initial geometry to recover one deforming volume mesh. Preserve material correspondence, avoid inverted elements, and explain regional wall motion and thickening.

2 · Physiology that can be checked

Recover directional strain, regional timing, twist and chamber mechanics with explicit references. Score image agreement, motion, strain and local volume change separately.

3 · A whole-heart research target

Extend to four coupled chambers, annuli and valve leaflets, active fibers and circulation. Each addition needs matching anatomy, motion or pressure/flow data. These components are not silently filled in here.

This workbench separates simulator reference playback, public-input reconstruction and privileged representation controls. The tissue reconstruction uses image observations, spatial smoothness and finite-volume regularization; it does not solve active cardiac force balance. Colors are interpolated for playback; numerical scores use the original 30 frames. Twist is an exploratory material rotation about the fixed reference long axis, not a clinical torsion measurement. The reference itself has local volume changes and a nonzero end-to-start motion gap.
