Provenance and coordinate contract

Reuses BR-030 source sampling, parallel-transport frames and surface preview. CTA: ImageCAS case 1. Annotation-derived routes: ImageCAS-X, R-PDA label 10 and R-PLA label 11. CTA voxel spacing is 0.377 × 0.377 × 0.5 mm. Coordinates are RAS mm under the input NIfTI affine. Model point bytes are copied unchanged. Arc axes are recomputed from those saved points. Review archives contain floating-point HU and source RAS coordinates for every CPR pixel, at 8 rotations and 0.25 mm offsets.

Reference-assisted mesh is simplified for display. Native CTA and numerical CPR values are not simplified. No mask was supplied to these model attempts. All assets are local; no remote services or model calls are made.
