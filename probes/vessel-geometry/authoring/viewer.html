What this demonstrates

The displayed repair is reference-assisted: 205 voxels added, 17 removed, no changes outside the 8 mm review sphere. The model prediction is untouched in the input. Segmentation components change from three to two after repair.

This is one training-split development case, not a held-out accuracy result. The released centerlines and meshes depend on the annotations. No lesion-level stenosis or plaque ground truth was supplied; these views support geometric review, not a validated diagnosis.

Sources and experiment details

CTA: ImageCAS case 1. Annotation, named centerlines, surface and pretrained CAS-Net: ImageCAS-X release, paper. The selected scan has excellent image quality and right dominance. The source supplies a binary disease descriptor, not lesion-specific severity labels.

CPR uses a parallel-transport frame, 45° rotation increments, 0.25 mm offsets and approximately 0.5 mm arc sampling. Images are windowed to −100…700 HU for display; numerical HU and RAS source coordinates are retained in the archive. Raw evidence and the frozen verifier remain local.
