01 / The task

Find the tissue
under the wrong name.

Give the agent existing organ masks, their proposed names, full CT and tools to render views. Ask which masks contain at least 5 mL of another organ.

Here, part of the source pancreas is transferred into its neighbor’s label. The remaining pancreas and the enlarged duodenum both retain plausible overall shapes. The transfer spans 45 × 37.5 × 67.5 mm.

A small, exact deliverable

Report the host mask, the included organ, and one point inside the wrongly assigned tissue. The point tolerance is 3 mm. No contour reconstruction is required.

The error is synthetic and its identity comes from the source mask. Original CT and total foreground are preserved; the other 11 organ masks are unchanged.

03 / The agent at work

Measure. Look. Reconsider.

A reconstruction from public progress messages, executed commands, returned images and final answers. Interpretations are marked separately.

1. Read the scene

Existing masks, proposed names, coordinates and CT.

2. Measure and render

Python geometry and intensity checks; targeted image sheets.

3. Assess anatomy

Compare shape, continuity and neighbors; request further views.

4. Return a finding

Host, included organ and interior point—or an empty list.

The model acts as a controller of this tool loop. No specialist medical inference model or new training was invoked in these traces. Existing segmentations were supplied; Sol’s task was to audit their ownership.

04 / What this suggests

Inspect ownership beyond plausible shape.

Coverage is a useful lead.

M02 saw the relevant pair and accepted its appearance. F01 called the global shapes coherent too, then continued into finer interface checks and found the inclusion. Narrower scope supplies an attention cue.

The negative control shows revision.

N01 publicly asserted two errors before later clearing the case correctly. That makes the additional checks and willingness to revise as interesting as the final score.

More images is not a recipe.

The focused pass received 17 images versus 27 in the broad miss. It took 13% less agent time and 17% fewer input tokens, but produced 13% more output. Efficiency remains an experiment to test.

These selected, single-run contrasts do not establish a failure rate or isolate a causal mechanism. F01 was a declared post-result follow-up that explicitly narrowed the audit to the affected pair. No additional trials were run for this presentation.
