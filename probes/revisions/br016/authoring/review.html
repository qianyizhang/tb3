Experiment design & evidence

Real images from OpenNeuro ds003949 (CC0). No synthetic lesions or pixel edits. The model received full original and skull-stripped volumes, metadata, and a slice/MIP helper, without reference masks or lesion-centred crops.

Scoring requires one point per released weak annotation, with 1 mm tolerance and one-to-one matching; an empty list is correct for the negative case. Weak labels support coarse localization, not exact contours. All trials completed normally. Matching oracle/no-op controls and explicit negative controls passed.

Time is agent execution; output tokens include reasoning. Costs are harness estimates. Total: 19m 59s agent time, 32,477 output tokens, approximately $3.58. Setup and verification bring model-trial wall time to 23m 20s; curation and separate controls are excluded.

Workflow diagrams summarize observable commands, generated views, public updates and final answers. They are not a reconstruction of private reasoning. Historical traces, reviews and frozen inputs are preserved.
