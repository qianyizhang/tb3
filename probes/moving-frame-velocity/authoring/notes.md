# BR-002/D01 authoring

The public helper composes explicit elementary rotations. Author truth uses
rotation generators and the product rule; the permitted baseline uses a
five-point finite difference of the pose. A further check differentiates the
entire transformed analytic world trajectory at multiple step sizes. Twelve
physical tracks have origin-shifted equivalents, giving 24 correlated cases.

The original source 041 describes Cartesian direction vectors and rotational
geometry between near-Earth frames. Sol's archived failures concern origin,
linearity, Gram matrices and round trips. They do not demonstrate confusion
about the coordinate derivative in this new moving-frame pilot.

Sources: [Astropy velocity transformations](https://docs.astropy.org/en/stable/coordinates/velocities.html).
No astrophysical data or historical task implementation is included here.
