02 / Inputs and coordinate audit

The agent had the volume. The grader used physical 3D distance.

These are coding-agent tasks: the agent can load the complete scan and render arbitrary slices, measure intensities and run image-processing tools. The model inspects rendered images through its image tool; this is not a native 3D tensor encoder. The final tasks supply identical NPY/NIfTI volumes, explicit shape, spacing, orientation and affine, and a checked three-plane viewer.

Answers use fractional, zero-based native [i,j,k] indices, explicitly tagged voxel_ijk_zero_based. Distance is the norm of the voxel difference transformed by the affine’s linear part, in millimetres. Screenshot coordinates, RAS/LPS physical coordinates and atlas coordinates are not interchangeable.

Independent SimpleITK and nibabel checks agreed on reference positions. Tests covered axis swaps, world coordinates submitted as voxels, wrong tags, crop offsets and single-voxel physical displacements. Oracle/no-op controls passed; saved submissions were independently rescored. Before/after frozen hashes and matching task checksums protected the final comparison.

Why the earlier results were investigated

BR-036 began with four PDDCA CT landmarks and eight AFIDs MRI points, then added cropped CT and 32-point MRI. Terra met 1/4 full-CT query rules and 0/8 full-MRI rules. Cropped CT met 2/4 by correctly rejecting both outside targets; cropped MRI met 0/32 and claimed all seven outside targets were inside. The original tasks also supplied full arrays, and raw sessions confirmed actual image inputs.

The BR-038 audit found no evaluator-wide coordinate conversion bug. A fresh native-voxel protocol produced 2/4 CT and 3/32 MRI points within 5 and 3 mm, respectively. Poor subject localization persisted after making the coordinate contract explicit. Changes in prompts, point sets and attempts prevent attributing the retest difference solely to the coordinate format.

03 / Query beyond the scan

False presence, missed presence and uncertainty need separate counts.

The expanded CT task asks for 26 vertebral centres: C1–C7, T1–T13 and L1–L6. The full VerSe scan has 24 source-labelled centres and no T13/L6 under its numbering convention. Cropping the same native volume leaves 13 centres visible and 11 outside. This tests unavailable targets without inventing annotations or treating unlabelled anatomy as absent.

Responses distinguish observed, out of field of view, absent and uncertain. Explicitly labelled outside estimates are permitted and evaluated separately. “Hallucination” here means claiming an unavailable target is observed inside the scan. Abstention avoids that false claim but does not earn a correct rejection.

Terra’s partial-CT false T4 point lay 2.14 mm from the real T5 centre: a wrong-level detection on real anatomy. Sol made no false observed claims, but incorrectly rejected visible T5 and abstained on T13. Because the crop lacks a cranial counting anchor, global enumeration can be ambiguous; uncertainty is meaningful. MRI in the final comparison contains all 32 targets, so it provides no MRI hallucination endpoint.

Dataset curation and the four-point CT limitation

PDDCA’s small illustrated head-and-neck point set limited the first CT pilot. A MedPelvis candidate offered 57 pelvic landmarks, but the audited case’s label-to-image correspondence did not pass inspection; it was held before any trial rather than repaired by guesswork. The completed expansion used independently checked VerSe vertebral references instead.

VerSe sub-verse823 supplies the native CT and source centroids; source numbering and the segmentation were checked separately. Its float64-to-int16 storage conversion preserved every voxel exactly, with no resampling. AFIDs SNSX sub-C001 supplies a T1 MRI and 32 consensus fiducials; the three-rater mean matched the released reference. Candidate sources, selection rules and rejected-source evidence remain in the round documents.

04 / What Sol used

MRI improvement included permitted atlas assistance.

Sol downloaded AFIDs protocol illustrations and a labelled generic MNI template, registered it affinely to the subject and refined points through image review. B-spline refinement failed and then terminated without reported proposal files. The final normal completion therefore supports atlas-assisted performance, not unaided visual localization or successful deformable registration.

The preserved command and downloaded-archive audit found no target-subject annotation retrieval. The AFIDs index exposed dataset URLs, but its downloaded archive held submodule metadata rather than subject FCSV files. Public-source training exposure remains unknown. Both models were allowed general references and software; Sol’s resource use is part of the observed method.

06 / Scope and retained record

A small capability probe, not a population estimate.

The series contains eight Terra/high and three Sol/xhigh attempts, all normally completed, across three source subjects. The final matched comparison contains one CT subject with correlated full/cropped views and one MRI subject. There are no repeat attempts, patient-level confidence intervals or independently adjudicated clinical outcomes. Model, reasoning effort and chosen methods differ. The coordinating agent curated inputs, wrote controls, scored outputs and authored this report; trial agents produced their submissions.
