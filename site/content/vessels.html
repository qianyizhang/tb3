02 / From one mask to an inspectable route

More deliverables made the result useful—and exposed new checks

The compact decision

Given an angiographic crop and a predicted mask, repair a broken branch, remove a false connection, or preserve a supported variant. Score the intended branch connection, agreement with the reference route, and edits elsewhere.

TopCoW supplies a benchmark motivation and public images with labels. Its published topology errors are external segmentation results; they are not local coding-agent failures. Merging named labels can also hide class-specific errors.

The larger workflow

Start from a named parent vessel, trace the intended distal branch, and export a corrected mask, ordered centerline, eight rotated curved planar reformations (CPRs), source coordinates and a world-space mesh.

A CPR unfolds an image plane along a curved route. Rotating that plane exposes different sides of the lumen. The route, sampled intensities and distance axis must agree; a convincing surface alone cannot establish this.

Clinical motivation is vessel-following review. These experiments do not have stenosis, plaque, flow or bronchoscopy-reachability ground truth. Mesh construction and CPR export add engineering work; they do not by themselves add anatomical difficulty.

03 / First feasibility test · BR-025 → BR-026

A synthetic repair passed. The preservation miss stayed disputed.

Four TopCoW MRAs were curated across bilateral, unilateral and absent Pcom annotations. Two became frozen tasks: a 200-voxel authored deletion in MRA 007 and an unchanged asymmetric reference mask in MRA 012. Terra restored 198 deleted voxels without collateral edits. An ordinary image-guided author baseline passed both tasks.

The unchanged-case output added 244 voxels beside the left anterior cerebral artery while preserving the annotated absent right Pcom. This fails the frozen preservation limits. The added region follows visible MRA signal, so that score alone does not identify invented anatomy.

What later adjudication did—and did not—resolve

Paired CTA/MRA landmark alignment had a 0.711 mm median and 1.362 mm maximum residual. It could not settle the small disputed structure. All 244 added voxels also remain background in the expanded TopBrain annotation. This reinforces reference disagreement, but is not independent clinical adjudication. No clinician was contacted and no true false-bridge failure is claimed.

04 / Real prediction error · BR-030

The anatomy passed. The saved distance axis did not.

A released CAS-Net checkpoint produced an approximately 5 mm internal RCA-to-R-PDA gap on ImageCAS-X case 1 despite 94.14% whole-mask Dice. The CTA showed contrast-filled lumen through the gap. The task used an unchanged crop of that prediction, an 8 mm review sphere and two route landmarks.

Terra added 103 voxels, changed nothing outside the review sphere, and achieved local Dice 0.9448 with centerline p95 distance 0.4996 mm. Repair, tracing and the closed mesh passed. The CPR sampled the correct source positions and HU values, but kept distances from the earlier voxel skeleton after resampling its centerline.

An 8.891 mm overstatement. Replacing only arc_mm with cumulative distances along the saved line makes the unchanged verifier pass.

The corrected package is an author diagnostic intervention, not a second model success. The original output and failure remain retained. The image-guided author method improves local overlap compared with the matched geometry-only repair/trace method (0.951 versus 0.900 Dice), but both pass. Image evidence was therefore not necessary to pass this fixture.

Case 1 is public training data. Named centerlines and meshes derive from corrected masks, so they are related references rather than independent anatomical votes. Full task, controls, trial and correction.

05 / User review corrected the interpretation · BR-033

“The disconnection is obvious.” The objection was right.

Three AeroPath CTs were screened with released Raidionics airway models. The frozen task bundled one natural omission (A01) with two preservation requests (A02/A03). Terra passed all requested route and CPR checks. The initial presentation called the controls intact, even though their parent attachments were still broken.

06 / Back to the first-choice source · BR-033

Five brain scans screened; the small repair remained a calibration

The initial TopBrain checkpoint download failed. It was subsequently recovered and checksum-verified. One released ResEncM fold-4 component ran on five full native MRAs: 004, 006, 007, 011 and 012. This was not the official three-model ensemble. No synthetic defect, reference-derived inference crop or pruning was applied.

Why no hard case was admitted

The two selected third-A2/A3 variants were already present and fully attached through the selected carotid-to-ACA chain. Three apparent new PCA–SCA face contacts already touched in the references under 26-neighbor connectivity. A reference MCA attachment was itself incomplete.

These candidates either passed the intended anatomy check or lacked a clean topology oracle. The screen was targeted development work, not an exhaustive review or a held-out performance estimate.

What was actually repaired

MRA 004 had a detached 247-voxel right superior cerebellar artery (R-SCA) fragment, with nearest voxel centers 0.939 mm apart. A geometry-only nearest-point bridge added two voxels and restored attachment to the basilar artery.

One addition matches R-SCA in the reference; the other is reference background. The full labeled output has no other changes. The displayed surface covers only the basilar artery and right SCA.

07 / Closeout

Useful reconstruction. Unestablished anatomical difficulty.

The original discrimination hypothesis remains untested. We have working natural-gap repair and geometry exports, a narrow reproducible CPR metadata miss, and a clear example of a passing grader checking too little. We do not have a confirmed difficult choice between a false connection and a genuine anatomical variant.

A future candidate must first survive simple geometry, use an independently defensible reference, and test attachment from the real parent. Intact and absent-branch controls need their own image-supported adjudication. That is an admission standard, not a queued experiment.

This authorized series is complete. Raw predictions, original agent outputs, task freezes and earlier viewer versions remain local. The selected submission and other research branches keep their separate ownership.
