01 / Finding

Separate shape, tissue motion and function.

Complete segmentations let both fresh Sol attempts build their own moving tetrahedral mesh with good shape agreement and correct finite-strain calculations. Longitudinal and circumferential strain met the research targets. Local radial strain did not.

In the earlier clinical task, a smooth, image-driven cavity tracker stayed close to the reference surface on average, yet underestimated contraction severely. Its resulting functional-severity assessment was wrong on all three selected scans.

These results do not show that Sol is generally bad at segmentation. The clinical task supplied the initial cavity surface; it tested tracking from that initialization. The later mask experiment supplied geometry at every phase, changing what remained to infer.

Agreement with a surface does not uniquely determine which tissue point moved where. Numerical strain correctness, resemblance to reference tissue motion and physical plausibility need separate checks.

03 / Final controlled comparison · BR-035

Supply the shape. Ask Sol to construct the moving body.

Both tasks supplied the same 30 complete 3D myocardial-wall masks on a 1.5 mm grid. The second added calibrated ultrasound. Neither supplied the source mesh, vertex identities, anatomical region labels, future tissue trajectories or reference strain. Sol built both meshes from voxel cells, registered the masks through time and regularized the deformation. The image-assisted method also used ultrasound texture.

Scientific figure and mechanics qualifications

Both submissions independently reproduce deformation-gradient, Green–Lagrange tensor and volume-ratio calculations within 2.3e−7 absolute error. That certifies their calculations from their coordinates, not the coordinates’ physiological accuracy. The interactive curves above use a different measure: directional engineering strain.

The two voxel-derived meshes have six nonmanifold boundary edges each. Their minimum local volume ratios, J, are 0.055 and 0.188. Only 0.1653% and 0.0014% of modeled reference tissue volume, respectively, has J<0.2 at the worst phase; this is an engineering indicator, not a clinical cutoff. No exhaustive self-intersection, global injectivity or force-equilibrium check was performed.

An analytic twist control has the same occupied shape as an untwisted cylinder but different strain. This demonstrates why complete masks cannot uniquely identify material motion. Agreement with a particular reference motion is therefore reported separately from the construction reward.

04 / Clinical value and its limits

A good-looking tracker can misstate heart function.

BR-034 selected a clearer real EchoXFlow examination after screening ten scans. It supplied full ultrasound volumes and the initial LV cavity surface. Subsequent surfaces and reference EF were hidden. Sol replaced the earlier fixed-table animation with image registration. Its unchanged executable passed still-input and phase-shift response controls.

All three average surface distances were below 3 mm, but contraction amplitude was substantially underestimated. The preserved-function control was falsely called reduced; all three functional-severity categories failed. Sol’s initial visual impression of mild-to-moderate reduction in the primary case was closer to the reference than its later severe assessment based on the erroneous model.

What happened when the complete segmentation was supplied?

BR-035 replayed both new executables unchanged on the primary clinical case with all-phase cavity masks. Both reached Dice ≈0.981 and EF 45.33%, versus 45.31% from the source surfaces. The tiny discrepancy comes from rasterizing those surfaces into 1.5 mm masks.

This is successful geometry preservation, not independent EF discovery. All-phase masks already encode the volume change, and both methods explicitly enforce those volumes. Both correctly declined to call cavity deformation myocardial strain: no myocardial wall or independent tissue-motion truth was available. These are different inputs from the BR-034 tracking task, so the EF contrast is not a matched algorithmic improvement.

Full-cycle segmentations supply much more geometry than an initial contour. Sparse/single-view reconstruction needs explicit assumptions and uncertainty; a unique unseen 3D completion should not be presumed. Future acceptance should add boundary-manifold and calibrated distortion checks, retaining these completed scores unchanged.

06 / Scope, authorship and provenance

Eight attempts. No pooled capability or clinical accuracy claim.

BR-031 contributes four fresh attempts, BR-032 one, BR-034 one and BR-035 two. All completed normally. Author prototypes, oracle/static controls and unchanged-code replays are separate evidence. One attempt per condition and selected cases do not establish failure rates, a model ranking or a general capability ceiling.

Who built what, source exposure, and retained evidence

The coordinating agent curated sources, authored controls and verifiers, ran the experiments, independently scored artifacts and built the labs/report. Sol and Terra authored their trial submissions. The original synchronized Dynamic Heart Lab included simulator ground-truth motion and author reconstructions; smooth playback alone was never a blind recovery result.

STRAUS supplies simulated material motion. FeEcho4D supplies fetal images and annotation-derived geometry. EchoSlicer supplied the initial real volunteer scan without reconstruction truth. EchoXFlow supplies real clinical cavity references with annotation/model uncertainty. These datasets do not jointly constitute one matched patient with whole-heart, valve, strain and flow truth.

All scans are public, so prior training exposure remains unknown. Reviewed model traces show no external reference retrieval or delegation. Hiding source names, withholding planes, changing coordinates and testing altered inputs reduce some reuse opportunities; they cannot prove absence from training. No pathology cause was independently diagnosed.

Source/reference controls, analytic checks, independent regrades, frozen input hashes, normal-completion receipts and infrastructure failures remain in the archive. The final pair passed construction controls; both missed the radial material target. Posthoc mesh defects are reported without changing frozen scores.
