## Value

## Evaluation

This conditional diagnostic isolates candidate judgment after an omission; it is a fresh agent task with privileged localization assistance, not full-volume detection.

## Difficulty

The centers remove discovery for this candidate. Its outcome-informed selection and remaining judgment work prevent comparison as a full-volume detection trial.
