# Revised WSI diagnostics with Sol 6 xhigh

The user accepted five design decisions on 2026-09-23: a corrected Sol-only round; HuBMAP reference recall with unmatched points queued for review; TIGER with and without source tissue masks and explicit class semantics; CAMELYON16 lesion-group localization on two positive and one negative slide; and HiESD six-class classification on annotated patches. These form **seven conditions across four task families**. All seven `openai/gpt-6-sol` xhigh attempts completed through the isolated WSI sidecar route. Every answer met the Harbor file contract (`reward=1`), but that reward is not the diagnostic score. The [evidence receipt](evidence/wsi-sol6-xhigh-v2-diagnostic-synthesis.json) pins the task digests, attempt IDs, raw traces, answers, and verifier output.

## Method and controls

All task images, GT-free overviews, prompts, answer contracts, separate verifier images, and solver images were frozen under the four [experiment protocols](../experiments/wsi-hubmap-inventory-v2-sol6-xhigh/protocol.md), [TIGER](../experiments/wsi-tiger-context-v2-sol6-xhigh/protocol.md), [CAMELYON](../experiments/wsi-camelyon-lesion-v2-sol6-xhigh/protocol.md), and [HiESD](../experiments/wsi-hiesd-patches-v2-sol6-xhigh/protocol.md). The same-image, same-Compose bounded Sol xhigh route toy passed before medical dispatch. Every current task digest had an oracle pass and a no-op contract failure. An earlier HuBMAP no-op exposed a verifier exception on missing answer files; that execution error remains recorded, the scorer was repaired, evaluator images were repinned, and the current controls were rerun. It was not a model result.

HuBMAP used the native PAS slide, a GT-free overview and level-0 crop helper. The evaluator matches one point per source polygon, accepting a point inside the polygon or within 50 µm of its edge. The agent tiled the slide, built a dark-round candidate detector, reviewed candidate and zoom sheets, and submitted 110 centers with notes for uncertain or altered profiles. Its agent phase took about 17.5 minutes. The 13 missed released polygons are misses against the present source reference. The 24 unmatched points require review because the source has no sclerosis subtype, partial-profile flag, or complete inclusion rule; the score intentionally does not call them false positives.

TIGER supplied identical source ROI images in both conditions; only the paired condition added the official tissue masks. The revised prompt defined all tissue codes, including code 6 as inflamed tumor-associated stroma. The evaluator matches cells one-to-one within 20 ROI pixels and measures compartment code at both the matched source-cell center and the submitted center. It retains a 20-pixel mask-boundary stratum. Image-only matched 428/518 cells overall; mask-supplied matched 432/518, but the ROI changes were −6, −17 and +27. Among the matched cells, source-center compartment correct counts were 11/11, 130/158 and 247/259 without masks, versus 5/5, 141/141 and 285/286 with masks. On the mask-supplied ROI3 output, all 286 submitted-center codes agreed with the mask there; one disagreed at the nearby matched source center. This is the exact boundary convention the revised evaluator exposes. The mask carries the evaluated tissue labels, so its attribution result is assisted lookup, not independent tissue inference. Different candidate detection scripts and one run per arm prevent a causal claim about detection.

CAMELYON used native TIFFs, GT-free overviews and level-0 crop helpers. The study groups released tumor polygons separated by at most 50 µm into lesion units: six polygons become four groups on `tumor_091`; 54 become 30 on `tumor_084`. The negative source has zero tumor polygons. This is a declared study rule, not the official CAMELYON challenge FROC. Both positive traces reviewed low-resolution slide regions and selected native crops, then judged suspicious pale areas non-metastatic and submitted empty lists. A post-hoc check found explicit native helper crops containing 2/4 oracle group points on `tumor_091` and 11/30 on `tumor_084`. Direct pyramid reads were also possible, so these are lower bounds on image access, not proof of visual attention. Sparse detail sampling is one supported inefficiency; failure to recognize tumor in sampled locations remains possible. A WebSocket-to-HTTPS fallback occurred in the `tumor_091` trace, but the attempt continued and completed. Representative **post-hoc** reference-centered crops from both positive slides show the annotated regions at native resolution and provide no obvious evidence of a corrupted coordinate mapping or empty GT. This visual review is not pathologist adjudication and cannot certify the source labels.

HiESD gave the agent twelve 256 × 256 target patches, 1024 × 1024 context crops, the full slide and a GT-free overview. Two patches were selected per XML class from one slide, at centers with pure 5 × 5 coarse-grid reference support and tissue-rich thumbnail neighborhoods. The agent made contact sheets, read wider context, consulted public histology descriptions, and labeled every patch. The four errors were `patch_02` reference tub1 → incomplete intestinal metaplasia; `patch_04` chronic gastritis → complete intestinal metaplasia; `patch_09` incomplete intestinal metaplasia → tub1; and `patch_11` complete intestinal metaplasia → normal glands. The two tub1/intestinal-metaplasia inversions indicate a consequential morphology distinction, but tiny target squares and region-level source labels warrant blinded pathology review before alleging a GT defect. The public web searches in the trace concerned general histology and dataset descriptions; no private `tests/` or `solution/` access appears in inspected commands. Public-data pretraining exposure remains unknown.

## Attribution and limits

- **Runtime:** the seven medical attempts completed. The route toy and current exact-task controls passed. The recovered CAMELYON stream fallback did not change its completed status. The earlier missing-answer verifier error remains a separate no-verdict record.
- **Agent:** HuBMAP still missed 13 released objects after a broad candidate sweep. TIGER cell localization varied by ROI and between runs. Both positive CAMELYON answers were empty despite nonempty source annotations; limited native detail coverage and cautious interpretation are visible in traces. HiESD confused four target patches despite supplied locations.
- **Specification/reference:** the revised contracts now distinguish HuBMAP recall from unadjudicated extras, reveal TIGER's two center conventions, group CAMELYON polygons into declared lesion units, and confine HiESD scoring to GT-selected annotated tissue. None requires reconstructing a hidden polygon boundary to submit a valid answer. No incorrect source GT label has been demonstrated. HuBMAP profile inclusion and the HiESD tub1/metaplasia distinctions remain candidates for blinded specialist review.

These are selected public slides or ROIs, with one stochastic attempt per condition. The normal control does not estimate specificity; the two positives do not estimate sensitivity; the TIGER contrast does not isolate a mask effect on cell detection; and HiESD's 12 patches do not estimate slide-level accuracy. The useful next evidence would be independent positive and negative slides with a fixed search budget, replicated TIGER attempts with identical detection procedure, and blinded adjudication of HuBMAP extras and ambiguous HiESD patches. Frozen answers and scores should remain unchanged.
