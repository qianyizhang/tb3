## Reference fitness and reopening

The [source study](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12021-022-09597-0) describes specialist
annotation review and weak enclosing spheres. Native-grid and independent scorer
checks favor a reference disagreement over a coordinate or packaging explanation
for N01. They do not independently establish clinical visibility/completeness.
Keep the reference-label miss; its perceptual/anatomical cause remains unresolved.

Clinical interpretation needs appropriate review of source labels and matched
native views. A future isolated negative condition needs source-exposure controls;
it is not authorized by this replay. Three selected cases and unknown training
overlap do not establish population accuracy, specialist-tool superiority or a
causal benefit from a particular heuristic. Frozen scores and setup-failure
evidence stay unchanged. The current explainer uses actual source pixels and
explicit reference reveals.
