# Historical source incidents and four passing calibrations

BR-003 recovered bounded source leads and authored four distinct prototypes. **One Terra/high attempt passed each prototype**. Those results do not establish failure on the original incidents; authentic vector-crop ownership remained untested. This is an assistant-authored retrospective audit, not a new research decision or model run.

## Retrieval and claim classification

The read-only [audit](../presentation/sources/history-sourcing-audit.json) recounts **395 indexed records − 142 automatic reviews − 16 other long-title records = 237 navigable records**. All 142 automatic reviews also had long titles; subtracting the full 158 long-title count again would double-count them. Nine conversation exports were selected. Eight retained excerpts come from six of those sessions.

Every excerpt matches its exact original JSONL record hash (excluding the line ending), recorded role and literal substring. E01–E03 contain PDF crop reports and an assistant diagnosis; E04–E08 contain resident lifecycle, queued work and stale-ledger reports. A byte-authenticated assistant diagnosis is still a historical diagnosis, not independently adjudicated failure. None of these eight records is a BR-003 benchmark trial.

H03 was a user-added task proposal. H04 was inspired by user recollection of SVG difficulty; the exact original SVG incident was not recovered. The recalled verdict-timeout/reroute incident was also not recovered, although related lifecycle evidence was retained. A prior title-only SVG screen found no additional original incident. This explainer checks the receipt-selected records without launching another broad history search.

## Task translation and reference limits

- **H01 changes the crux.** Fixed-document table curation is related to the crop reports, but does not test authentic vector-diagram crop ownership. Its geometry gate requires at least 0.97 reference coverage and at most 1.20 box-to-reference area. The original vector/prose boundary question remains H05.
- **H02 is deterministic simulation.** Deadlines at or before the current timestamp expire before the event. Full assignment identity, restart handling, idempotent retransmission and room ordering are explicit rules. No real messaging connection, external availability or wall clock is tested.
- **H03 is bounded gross QA.** Five packets derive from one CT, not five independent patients. No public anatomical rule list was supplied. The intermediate row/column affine error was repaired before the passing final result; it is not a final model failure. This audit does not clinically adjudicate the anatomical reference.
- **H04 is supplied-mask geometry.** It shares the source CT with H03. Pixel-centre sampling and nearest-voxel rules are explicit; raster content inside SVG is permitted. Success does not establish unaided anatomical drawing. The original container was not retained; byte checks cannot establish recovery of that runtime.

## Controls and retained provenance

Targeted author controls reject oversized PDF boxes (area ratios 2.9799 and 2.7248), accepting chat results at their deadline (five named failing traces), requiring a heart outside partial scan coverage, and mirrored/transposed/bounding-box SVG masks. These demonstrate specified grader discrimination and complement solvable oracle and no-op controls. They do not establish a difficult task or additional model failures.

The new read-only utility verifies **182 pinned sources**, **76 frozen source files**, **four exact snapshot archives** and all **twelve raw result records** against retained receipts. It inspects archive bytes in memory without extracting or executing historical authoring modules. Task-level checksums match the oracle, no-op and model records within each task. Historical whole-directory checksums include ignored caches; a clean source checkout can differ despite matching all frozen source files.

The native `anatomy-audit-br003` experiment index retains only the six medical executions. The [record evidence manifest](evidence/history-sourcing-calibration.json) therefore covers that native subset. The separate full-round audit pins the archived PDF/chat contracts, four freezes and all twelve original outcomes; the native index is not presented as full-round coverage.

## Interpretation and disposition

Four passing calibrations answer four deliberately bounded contracts. Different endpoints cannot be pooled into accuracy, and single attempts do not support population claims or causal model rankings. The medical conditions share source data. Original historical incidents involve different contexts and models. H05 remains an untested source gap.

The historical round remains closed; the [patient-plane idea and parking decision](../ideas/idea-dicom-triplanar-svg.md) are unchanged. No new trial, saved-code replay, publication, runtime recovery or clinical certification is implied. Source-derived highlights in the explainer traverse records, not an observed solver search. The delayed private-outcome table is a reader reveal; it does not alter the original solver's access.
