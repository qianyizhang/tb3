Extract and anatomically label the visible coronary artery centerline trees from the CTA at `/app/data/image.nii.gz`. Discover both trees and their branches from the image alone. No target branch, mask, seed, landmark, centerline, or list of branches present in this patient is provided. This is an exploratory image geometry task, not diagnosis.

Scope: all visible coronary arteries represented by the following vocabulary. Do not trace aorta, pulmonary vessels, or veins. Labels: 1 LM (left main), 2 LAD, 3 LCx, 4 D1, 5 D2, 6 OM1, 7 OM2, 8 IM (ramus intermedius), 9 RCA, 10 R-PDA, 11 R-PLA, 12 L-PDA, 13 L-PLA, 14 Other (additional diagonal/obtuse marginal branches). Use anatomical origin/course to assign labels. Not every category is necessarily present. Follow visible lumen centrally and stop when continuation is not adequately supported; describe uncertain distal extent. Do not invent absent branches to fill the vocabulary.

Output `/app/answer/centerlines.json` as {"centerlines": [{"id": "unique name", "points_ras_mm": [[x,y,z],...], "labels": [integer,...]}, ...]}. Each polyline has one label per point. Split at bifurcations when practical; labels may change within a polyline. Cover the complete trees without repeatedly exporting shared proximal trunks. Polylines must follow continuous lumen, with 2–20000 finite points each, nonzero steps no larger than 1.5 mm (approximately 0.5 mm preferred), at most 200 polylines total. Coordinates are RAS millimeters, using the NIfTI affine on zero-based XYZ voxel indices. The image has native HU values and original field of view.

Write `method.md` documenting method, branch inventory, uncertainties, external assistance and compute used. Save your extraction code in the answer directory for reproducibility. Optional segmentation or figures are welcome; CPR and mesh are not required.

Evaluation uses private annotation-derived labeled centerlines. It reports arc-weighted geometric coverage, same-label coverage per category, and submitted length agreeing with the reference, at 1 mm (2 mm diagnostic). A predeclared annotation-agreement pass requires mean per-present-label recall >=90%, each present label recall >=80%, and overall same-label precision >=90%, all at 1 mm. Points beyond annotation extent lower precision; that disagreement is not independently proof of false anatomy. No exact distal endpoint or length-ratio gate is used. The label inventory present in this case stays private. This single public development case does not establish clinical completeness or generalization.

NumPy, SciPy, nibabel, Pillow, scikit-image and trimesh are installed. Use image inspection and ordinary tools/general methods. Do not retrieve this case's annotations, masks, centerlines or prior solutions, or use a pretrained coronary model whose training overlap with this public case is unknown.
