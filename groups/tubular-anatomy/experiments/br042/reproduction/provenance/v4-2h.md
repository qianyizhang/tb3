## Interpretation

The evidence supports an agent capable of constructing and documenting a substantial exploratory vessel reconstruction from CTA alone, reviewing uncertain connections, and producing reproducible geometry conditional on its own reviewed specifications. Persistent small-branch omissions, numbering dependencies and identity/boundary disputes limit the coronary result. Doubling the allowance did not yield normal completion or outperform the completed medium run on this case. The appropriate next analysis separates anatomical adjudication, blind discovery and workflow/time management rather than collapsing all three into one score.
