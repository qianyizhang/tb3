Original clinical CT from Radboud University Medical Center, Nijmegen, The Netherlands. Challenge data are resampled, cropped and affine prealigned. This task converts a scan pair to HU arrays and derives an oblique view and query positions. World coordinates are dataset coordinates, not asserted native scanner LPS.
