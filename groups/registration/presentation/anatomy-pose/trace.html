How the image maps into the volume

For each pixel column u and row v, the submitted 4 × 4 matrix maps [0.9·u, 0.9·v, 0, 1] into patient LPS millimetres. The volume’s voxel-to-LPS matrix then locates that point in the CT grid. Trilinear interpolation samples its intensity.

Image pixel→Patient point→CT voxel position→Rendered intensity

The solver changes six pose parameters, renders another section, and measures a mismatch. It returns the pose, not an organ mask or an anatomical diagnosis. 

No selected pose at the input stage.

Matrix translation is the top-left pixel centre, not the centre of the section. All geometry in this viewer uses the source LPS affine.

What this attempt establishes—and its limits

Observed: early local optima looked plausible; the logged silhouette stage found a much closer pose; full-resolution refinement produced the submitted matrix. Re-rendering that saved matrix reproduces the logged 56 differing pixels out of 36,864, with maximum intensity difference 1.

Supported interpretation: broad silhouette matching found a useful starting region for final intensity refinement. This is a reconstruction of one run, not an ablation proving that the silhouette change alone caused success. The solver also changed its search bounds and earlier objectives.

Reference fitness: the target was generated from this same CT. The frozen contract explicitly defines spacing, LPS axes, interpolation and the 49-point score; retained author controls support geometric consistency. It is not an expert-certified cardiac view. No new clinical reference adjudication was performed.

Scope: one attempt on one subject. Same-acquisition texture makes very precise numerical recovery possible. Both historical BR-019 snapshots passed and were retired as difficulty candidates. Cross-scan robustness and anatomical understanding are not established.

Replay limits: the intermediate matrices and coarse-search parameters were printed at limited precision. Their reslices are new reader-facing reconstructions; the first reconstructed image also matches the image actually delivered at trace step 18 pixel for pixel. We have selected states, not the complete optimizer history.
