# Explanation migration priorities

The [per-entry ledger](EXPLAINER-LEDGER.json) owns the baseline, reviewed status,
story bindings, source/input blockers and retained contract snapshots. Consult it
for entry and family inventories; do not maintain a second status table here.

## Ordered batches

1. Topology path/inventory, rigid correspondence and planar WSI slots/coverage.
2. Bounded edit/control, material ambiguity, longitudinal coverage and retained anatomy.
3. CT/MRI operators and WSI coordinate navigation.
4. Remaining internal conditions, other inverse problems/restoration/fields,
   reporting/data and external entries. Keep source blockers distinct from
   unfinished operation reviews.

## Completion boundary

A story binding requires its own integrated media and visual acceptance. Preserve
each task's actual inputs, helpers, outputs, conditions and reference boundaries;
a passing build or generic family story does not establish catalogue completion.
Retain acceptance receipts, source hashes and contract projections in the ledger.

Use [canonical explainers](EXPLAINERS.md) for authoring and runtime contracts and
[fixture retention](assets/teaching-fixtures/EXPANSION.md) for asset provenance.
