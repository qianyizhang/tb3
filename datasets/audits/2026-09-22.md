## Limits and remaining questions

The BR-040 MRI trial used the generic `MNI152NLin2009cAsym` TemplateFlow
image, but its native image bytes and SHA-256 were not retained in the audited
workspace. The retained archive contains a git-annex pointer plus landmark
tables, not that image. The dataset page and `unverified_payloads` audit field
keep this **one known image recovery gap** visible. Recovering an upstream copy
later would not by itself prove identity with the historical runtime image.

CT-RATE imaging was not acquired in the audited access screen. ACRIN brain
shortlisting used metadata without controlled images. Both have explicit records;
neither is presented as an acquired patient sample or model trial.

Byte identity does not adjudicate anatomical labels, clinical laterality,
reference completeness, training exposure or redistribution rights. Existing
TopCoW, dental, STS-3D, MedPelvis3D and cardiac reference qualifications remain in
the source pages. Terms and upstream availability were not refreshed online.

Completeness is scoped to registered medical experiments, retained source screens
and acquired external Task Explorer examples. External survey-only definitions,
every upstream benchmark case, abandoned downloads, and arbitrary unregistered
content in ignored runtime folders are not claimed as verified samples. New
selections in an existing experiment must still be documented explicitly.
